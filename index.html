    The game loop

    Promise of a rewardA reason to want to act
    Desired behaviorWhat the product wants
    Receiving the rewardValue created by the system’s rules
    Loopthe reward creates a reason to act again

    Example 01

    Duolingo league

    1. Promise: overtake competitors and move up to the next league
    2. Action: complete a lesson and earn XP
    3. Reward: move up the leaderboard

    Example 02

    Kinder Surprise

    1. Promise: the chance to find a toy you want
    2. Action: buy and open an egg
    3. Reward: get the toy and add it to your collection

    Example 03

    Supermarket loyalty stamps

    1. Promise: unlock a big discount on a high-value item
    2. Action: shop regularly and collect stamps
    3. Reward: buy the item for less

    Achievements

    • Improve retention
    • Deepen engagement
    • Encourage users to do more

    Rewards

    When users can see each other

    Status

    Profile frames, backgrounds, titles, leaderboard position, or winning a competition.

    Inside the product

    Progress

    An achievement, a new level, access to new content, or new customization options.

    Beyond the system

    Real-world value

    A discount, extra subscription time, or a physical item worth keeping.
